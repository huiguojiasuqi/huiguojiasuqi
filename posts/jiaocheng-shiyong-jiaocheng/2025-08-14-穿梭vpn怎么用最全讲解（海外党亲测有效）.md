### 为什么海外访问国内服务会受限？

这背后的原因其实并不复杂，主要是因为国内的许多内容和服务都存在着区域性的限制，也就是我们常说的“地域限制”或“区域限制”。这背后涉及到版权问题、服务器部署策略以及内容审核等多种因素。

#### IP 属地识别是关键

most of the time, services determine your location based on your IP address. If your IP address indicates you're outside of mainland China, they'll block or limit your access. This is often done through CDNs (Content Delivery Networks) which serve content from servers closest to your location. When you're abroad, your IP points to a foreign server, hence the restrictions.

#### 版权与运营策略

For content like music and videos, copyright agreements often dictate where certain content can be distributed. For example, a show might be licensed for broadcast only within China. Similarly, some services might have different feature sets or pricing tiers for different regions. Even government services like the 12123 platform are strictly designed for domestic users.

#### 特定服务的限制

Even for services that aren't strictly geo-restricted in the same way, things like payment systems, user authentication, or even server load balancing can be designed with a primarily domestic user base in mind. This can lead to unexpected issues when accessed from abroad.
